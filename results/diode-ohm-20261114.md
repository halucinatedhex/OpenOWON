# Diodes and ohms settling, 2026-11-14 (panel build)

## Diodes (`DIAG:DIODE`, factory cal)

| Part | Ours | Third meter |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1N4148 forward | 0.567 V (0.565-0.568) | 0.615 V |
| 1N4148 reversed | 3.27 V (open) | |
| Schottky | 0.15 V (0.148-0.157) | |
| Red LED | 1.49 V | |

The 48 mV gap on the 1N4148 is the meters' different test currents
(about a 2-3x ratio for that junction), not an error: the diode reading
goes through the DC V 5 V calibration, checked in T-DCV. The ±4 mV wander
is the diode row's fast rate (R22 0x10). Test current not measured.

## Ohms settling at the fast rate (`DIAG:ROW OHM r 20 22=10`)

Leads shorted read 0 ohm (counts -0.04 ohm, clamped): the factory zero
covers the leads.

- 10 kOhm, 50 kOhm range: settled by the 4th fast conversion (shown from
  the 4th): 10,000-10,010 vs 10,004 at full resolution.
- 47 kOhm, 50 kOhm range: settled at once: 47,141-47,155 vs 47,152.
- 47 kOhm, **500 kOhm range: decays from above**, 222k -> 190k -> 164k
  ... 66k after 12 conversions, each step removing ~18% of the remaining
  error. On the higher ranges the source settles over time; the 5 and 50
  MOhm ranges will be slower.

At the normal rate the 47 kOhm read 79k, 47.47k, then settled from the
third conversion (~0.5 s).

**For ohms' fast hunt:** wait until two fast readings in a row agree
(say within 2%) before deciding a range; that adapts to each range
without a table of settle times.
