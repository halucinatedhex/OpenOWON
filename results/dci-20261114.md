**Direct check, same day: the ranges really disagree.** Third meter on
DC volts across the 4.7 kOhm (4623.0 ohm on our 5 kOhm range), supply 20 V,
our meter in series:

| Our range | Ours | Volts across 4.7 k | V/R | Ours vs V/R |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 5 mA | 4.2476 mA | 19.81 V | 4.285 mA | **-0.9%** |
| 50 mA | 4.3411 mA | 19.81 V | 4.285 mA | **+1.3%** |

The voltage did not move when the range changed, so the current did not:
the mA shunts stay in series and the range only picks the tap. (An
earlier explanation in this file, that the 2% gap was the shunt burden
changing the current, was **wrong** and is withdrawn.) The third meter's
own current readings (4.23 mA, 104.3 uA) agree with our 5 mA range and so
look ~1% low against V/R; its DC volts tracked ours closely (T-DCV).

So ranges 0-1 (PA8 off) read ~0.9% low and ranges 2-3 (PA8 on) ~1.3%
high against V/R, a 2.2% split (2.1-2.2% in every comparison today). The
factory gains are 1.0126/1.0123 for 0-1 and 1.0236/1.0220 for 2-3. Likely
cause: the v2.1.2 rows tap the shunt chain differently from what this v3
meter's factory calibration (made under V4.3.0) was taken against, as
capacitance's R24 differed. Next: a constant current; then try row
variants on both pairs (PA13, R24 0x35/0xb5, R32) and look for the one
where ranges agree with V/R; the 10 A jack; then the app.

**Prepared for the next session (offline).** Ranges 0/2 and 1/3 have the
same registers and differ only in PA8; ranges 1 and 2 differ in R24
(0xb5/0x35) and R32. R24 is SAD1FP<3:0> | SDIO | SAD1FN<2:0> (datasheet):
0x35 = OP1O / PB<3>, 0xb5 = PB<3> / PB<3>. A 1-2% gain error between two
shunt taps is a contact or trace resistance, so the suspects are the PA13
relay and R24's negative tie point. `DIAG:ROW` now takes `PA8=x PA13=x`
as well as `RR=HH`; `results/dci-sweep.py <port> <amps>` runs the matrix
(baseline, PA13 on, six SAD1FN choices on each of ranges 1 and 2) at
~4.3 mA through the 4.7 kOhm and prints each against V/R.


## Sweep, same day (`dci-sweep-20261114.txt`, 4.2851 mA by V/R)

PA13 makes no difference (-0.90 / +1.24%). No SAD1FN tie point brings
both ranges onto V/R: range 1 stays -0.77 .. -0.95% on RLU, AGND, PB2,
PB3, PB4; range 2 scatters (PB4 +0.71%, PB2 -1.86%, RLU +1.53%). SENSE on
range 1 reads exactly 1/10 of V/R (0.42850 mA), ~10x on range 2: the
taps are clean and in proportion. VSSA reads garbage (rail offset).

The XDM2041 record has the same ~1.1% step between the current pairs
(DCI 1.0171/1.0168 -> 1.0279/1.0276), so the step is normal.

**Hypothesis: on this meter the two current calibration pairs are
swapped.** Range 1 with the 2/3 gain (x1.0236/1.0123): 4.2918 mA,
+0.16%; range 2 with the 0/1 gain (x1.0126/1.0236): 4.2905 mA, +0.13%.
The two land within 0.03% of each other and inside V/R's ~0.2%. One
current only: to test with V/R at ~100 uA (ranges 0-1) and ~40 mA
(ranges 2-3) before changing any mapping.
