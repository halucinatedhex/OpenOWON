**Reading.** Every step but the short is CHECK against the 0.1% limit.
The gap is a steady ~1% gain difference on the 50 V range, the second
meter low. Our readings sit within 0.03% of the supply's set points at
9, 12 and 30 V, an independent agreement that is unlikely by chance. An
earlier session (901c278) found the same direction: 8.2 V on a second
meter vs our 8.225 V. Conclusion: the second meter reads ~1%
low; ours agrees with the supply. Not a certified pass: neither the supply
nor the handheld is a calibrated reference. To settle it, a voltage
reference (LM4040/REF50xx 0.1%, or an AD584 board).
